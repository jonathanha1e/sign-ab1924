Tell Governor Newsom to sign AB 1924 and make homelessness prevention a state priority.

AB 1924 passed the Legislature with strong bipartisan support, and now it's on Governor Newsom's desk. He needs to hear from Californians like yourself to decide whether to sign this bill.

What the bill does

AB 1924, authored by Assemblymembers Jesse Gabriel (D-Encino) and Sharon Quirk-Silva (D-Fullerton) and co-sponsored by Inner City Law Center, requires the California Interagency Council on Homelessness to develop and publicly issue a statewide homelessness prevention strategy by July 1, 2027.

California has made historic investments in getting people housed, but has done far less to stop people from becoming homeless in the first place. Statewide, nearly three people fall into homelessness for every one person who gets housed. AB 1924 would push the state to treat prevention as a strategic priority, keeping people off the streets and saving taxpayer dollars.

"Preventing homelessness is so much less expensive and more humane than reacting to it. Yet for all of California's historic investments to address homelessness, the state has done very little to prevent it from happening in the first place."

— Mike Feuer, Senior Policy Advisor, Inner City Law Center
